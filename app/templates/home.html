About Axion

Organised intelligence for your balance sheet.

Axion turns the invoices you receive and send into clear, checked numbers. Upload a PDF or a scan, and Axion reads the supplier, dates, GSTIN, taxes and totals, checks them against GST rules, and keeps everything in one organised place. From there you can see who owes you, what you owe, and where your GST stands, without spreadsheets.
Capabilities

What Axion does

{{ icon('upload', size=22) }}

Invoice extraction

Reads PDFs and scanned invoices, including line items and HSN codes with high-precision OCR fallback.

{{ icon('shield', size=22) }}

GST validation

Checks GSTIN, state, tax split, standard slabs and arithmetic totals before mistakes reach your books.

{{ icon('check', size=22) }}

Review and approval

Correct anything side by side with the original, and only approved invoices count in reports.

{{ icon('calendar', size=22) }}

Payments and ageing

Track what is paid, due and overdue, with automated running ledgers for receivables and payables.

{{ icon('reports', size=22) }}

Reports and GST summaries

Monthly business report, accrual profit and loss, statutory GSTR-style summaries and an HSN breakdown.

{{ icon('exports', size=22) }}

Invoicing and exports

Create GST invoices as PDFs, and export to Tally XML, Excel, CSV and an accountant package, with backups.
